How growth builds on growth.

Explore compound interest, recall the idea, then apply it to a new situation.

Interest earns interest.

Simple interest adds the same amount each year. Compound interest adds a percentage of the current balance, so the amount added grows.

● Compound · – – Simple

Predict first: at a higher rate, will the gap between the curves grow or shrink?

See the formula and yearly values

Compound: A = P × (1 + r)t. Simple: A = P × (1 + r × t).

A is the final balance; P is the starting principal; t is years; r is the annual rate as a decimal (5% = 0.05).

Model: $1,000 starting balance, annual compounding, constant non-negative rate, no deposits, fees, tax or inflation. The chart scale stays fixed as you change the rate.
